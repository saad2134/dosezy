## 1. App Overview, 12 Languages & Dark/Light Themes

Dosezy is designed with accessibility and clarity as top priorities: large text, high-contrast buttons, clean layouts, and zero confusing menus.

### 12 Supported World Languages:
Dosezy is fully localized in **12 languages**:
- 🇬🇧 English, 🇪🇸 Spanish (Español), 🇮🇳 Hindi (हिन्दी), 🇨🇳 Chinese (中文), 🇵🇹 Portuguese (Português), 🇸🇦 Arabic (العربية), 🇫🇷 French (Français), 🇩🇪 German (Deutsch), 🇯🇵 Japanese (日本語), 🇷🇺 Russian (Русский), 🇮🇹 Italian (Italiano), and 🇧🇩 Bengali (বাংলা).

### Changing Language or Theme:
1. Tap the **Menu** tab at the bottom right.
2. Tap **Preferences**.
3. Select **Language** to choose your preferred language.
4. Select **Theme** to switch between **System Default**, **Light**, or **Dark** mode (with zero white-flash screen transitions).
5. Select **Time Format** to toggle between **12-Hour (AM/PM)** and **24-Hour** display.

## 2. Home Dashboard & Live Dose Status Badges

The **Home** screen displays all medications scheduled for today, grouped into clean chronological time sections (e.g., `08:00 AM`, `01:00 PM`, `08:00 PM`).

### Time Section Headers:
Below each time header, Dosezy shows real-time progress text:
- `To be taken in 45m`: An upcoming dose.
- `1h 15m ago`: A dose whose time has passed.
- `All medications taken`: All pills in this slot are completed.

### Medication Card Badges:
Each medicine card displays its name, dosage, meal instruction, and live status:
- 🟢 **Taken**: Green text with the recorded completion time (e.g. `Taken at 08:05 AM`).
- ⚪ **Standard Text (`To be taken in...` / `Xh Xm ago`)**: Dose is upcoming or due recently (within your "Consider Late After" window).
- 🟧 **Orange Text (`Xh Xm ago`)**: Dose is **Late** (3 to 6 hours past due by default). An orange **Mark Late** button will appear on the card.
- 🔴 **Red Text (`Xh Xm ago`)**: Dose is **Missed** (more than 6 hours past due by default).
- 📦 **Stock Badges**: Shows remaining units (e.g. `📦 Stock: 45`) or an orange **`⚠️ Refill Warning: 5 left`** badge when running low.

## 3. Adding & Editing Medications

### Adding a Medication:
1. Tap **Medicines** in the bottom navigation bar.
2. Tap the floating **"+"** button or **"+ Add Medicine"**.
3. Enter the **Medicine Name** (e.g., *Metformin*, *Aspirin*, *Lisinopril*).

### Medicine Photos & Food Instructions:
- **Medicine Photo**: Tap the camera icon to take a photo of your pill box/tablet or choose an image from your gallery with built-in cropping.
- **Meal Instructions**: Choose between:
  - 🍽️ *Before Food*
  - 🍲 *With Food*
  - 🥗 *After Food*
  - ⏱️ *No Food Instructions*

### Dosage Units & Frequency Patterns:
- **Dosage**: Enter the quantity (e.g. `1`, `500`) and select the unit:
  - `tablet`, `capsule`, `mg`, `mcg`, `g`, `mL`, `drops`, `puffs`, or `units`.
- **Frequency Pattern**:
  - **Daily**: Every day.
  - **Weekly**: Choose specific days of the week (e.g., Mon, Wed, Fri).
  - **Monthly**: Choose specific dates of the month (e.g., 1st, 15th).

### Multi-Dose Quick Presets (1x, 2x, 3x, 4x):
For medications taken multiple times per day, tap a quick preset chip to populate standard times automatically:
- **1x Daily**: `08:00 AM`
- **2x Daily**: `08:00 AM`, `08:00 PM`
- **3x Daily**: `08:00 AM`, `02:00 PM`, `08:00 PM`
- **4x Daily**: `08:00 AM`, `12:00 PM`, `04:00 PM`, `08:00 PM`

### 12-First Grid Time Picker:
Tap any dose time chip to open the interactive time selector:
- **12 is at the top**: Hour numbers start with 12 followed by 1 to 11 for natural morning/night selection.
- **AM / PM Toggle**: Fast 1-tap switching.
- **Quick Minute Chips**: Tap `:00`, `:15`, `:30`, or `:45`, or slide the minute bar for exact minute precision.

## 4. Slot-Based Grouped Alarms & "Take All"

If you take multiple tablets at the exact same minute (e.g., 3 different pills at 8:00 AM):
- Dosezy rings with a **single grouped full-screen alarm** instead of triggering multiple conflicting alerts.
- The screen shows user details, scheduled time, and an interactive checklist of every medicine in that time slot.
- **TAKE ALL (Green Button)**: Marks every medicine in the slot as taken simultaneously and deducts their stock counts.
- **SNOOZE (Orange Button)**: Pauses the reminder for 10 minutes.
- You can also check off individual tablets if you only took some of them.

## 5. Pill Inventory & Automatic Refill Warnings

Dosezy includes automatic inventory management so you never run out of critical medications:

1. In the Add/Edit Medicine screen, turn on **Track Stock Inventory**.
2. Enter your **Current Stock** count (e.g. `60` tablets).
3. Enter your **Refill Warning Threshold** (e.g. `10` tablets).
4. **Auto-Deduction**: Whenever you tap **Taken** (from alarms or the Home screen), Dosezy automatically subtracts your dosage amount from your stock.
5. **Low Stock Alerts**: When remaining stock reaches or drops below your threshold, an orange **`⚠️ Refill Warning: X left`** badge is displayed on your Home screen and Medicines list.

## 6. Interactive Schedule Calendar

Tap **Schedule** in the bottom navigation bar to view your full medication calendar:
- **Week & Month Views**: Tap any day (Mon–Sun) or date on the calendar to view past or future schedules.
- **Adherence Markers**: Colored dots on each calendar day show your adherence for that date (green for all taken, orange/red for missed doses).
- Review historical compliance logs at any time.

## 7. Adherence Analytics & Progress Scores

Tap **Analytics** from the Menu to view detailed compliance charts:
- **Time Range Selector**: View stats for **WEEK**, **MONTH**, **YEAR**, or **ALL** time.
- **Overall Score Ring**: Displays your total adherence percentage (e.g. `92% Adherence`).
- **Dose Breakdown**: Counts of **On-Time**, **Late**, **Missed**, and **Total** doses.
- **Medicine Breakdown**: See compliance rates for each specific medicine to identify which pills are frequently taken late or missed.

## 8. Customizing Late & Missed Thresholds

You can customize how much time passes before Dosezy flags a dose as late or missed:
1. Tap **Menu > Preferences**.
2. **Consider Late After**: Choose `1 hour`, `2 hours`, or `3 hours` (default is **3 hours**).
3. **Consider Missed After**: Choose between `3 hours` and `9 hours` (default is **6 hours**).
4. **Default Snooze Duration**: Choose 5, 10, 15, 20, or 30 minutes.

## 9. International Emergency Dialing & Personal Contacts

In an urgent situation, Dosezy provides rapid, 1-tap emergency calling without needing to look up emergency numbers:

1. Tap **Menu > Emergency Contacts**.
2. **Official Emergency Services**:
   - Dosezy automatically detects your country using your SIM network or system language.
   - You can also manually choose between 10 regions:
     - 🇮🇳 **India** (Ambulance: 108 | Fire: 101 | Police: 100 | Universal: 112)
     - 🇺🇸🇨🇦 **USA & Canada** (911)
     - 🇬🇧 **United Kingdom** (999 / 112)
     - 🇪🇺 **European Union** (112)
     - 🇨🇳 **China** (120 / 119 / 110)
     - 🇯🇵 **Japan** (119 / 110)
     - 🇷🇺 **Russia** (103 / 101 / 102 / 112)
     - 🇧🇷 **Brazil** (192 / 193 / 190)
     - 🇧🇩 **Bangladesh** (999)
     - 🇦🇺 **Australia** (000)
   - Tap any card to open your phone's dialer instantly (`ACTION_DIAL`).
3. **Personal Emergency Contacts**:
   - Tap **"+ Add Emergency Contact"** to save personal numbers (family doctor, primary caregiver, son, daughter, or neighbor).
   - Tap your saved contact to call them immediately in an emergency.

## 10. Managing Multiple Family Profiles

Manage medications for your whole household on a single phone:

1. Tap **Menu > Switch Profile**.
2. Tap **Add Profile** to create a profile for an elderly parent, child, or partner.
3. Enter their Name, Age, Gender, and optional Profile Picture.
4. Tap any profile card to switch active users. Each profile maintains its own separate medicine list, schedules, stock counts, and adherence history.

## 11. Exporting Doctor PDF Reports & Data Backups

Export health records to print for doctor appointments or keep personal backups:

1. Tap **Menu > Export Data**.
2. **PDF Report**: Generates a clean, professional medical report summarizing active prescriptions, dosage amounts, food instructions, stock levels, and adherence logs formatted for printing or emailing to your doctor.
3. **JSON Backup / CSV Export**: Creates a digital archive file that you can save to Google Drive or local storage to restore anytime.

## 13. Notification & Battery Optimization Troubleshooting

If medication reminders are silent or delayed on your Android device:

1. **Check Notification Status**: Open **Menu > Notification Status** (or tap the bell icon in the top bar) to view a live diagnostic checklist.
2. **Enable Notification & Exact Alarm Permissions**: Ensure both permissions are toggled ON in Android Settings.
3. **Disable Battery Optimization (Crucial)**: Many phone manufacturers put background apps to sleep. When Dosezy asks to ignore battery optimizations, tap **Allow** so your alarms ring on time even when your phone is locked.
