## 1. Getting Started

### Download and Installation
- Download Dosezy from the **GitHub** / **Google Play Store** (for Android).
- Open the app after installation.

### Initial Setup
1.  **Permissions:** You will be asked to allow notifications. This is crucial for receiving your medication reminders. Please select "Allow."
2.  **Local Data Storage:** All your medication data is stored securely directly on your device. There is no account creation required.

---

## 2. Your Dashboard

The Dashboard is your home screen, providing an at-a-glance view of your medication schedule.

- **Today's Schedule:** A clear list of all medications due today, sorted by time.
- **Status Indicators:**
    - `UPCOMING`: The dose is due soon.
    - `DUE NOW`: The dose is currently due.
    - `TAKEN`: You have logged the dose as taken.
    - `MISSED`: The reminder was not acted upon.
- **Quick Actions:** Easily log a dose as "Taken" or "Skipped" directly from the Dashboard.

---

## 3. Managing Medications

### Adding a Medication

1.  Tap the **"+"** (Plus) icon, usually located at the bottom or top right of your Dashboard.
2.  **Enter Details:**
    - **Name:** Type the medication name. The app will suggest matches from its database.
    - **Dosage:** Enter the strength and form (e.g., `10mg`, `1 tablet`, `5ml`).
    - **Schedule:** Set the frequency (daily, specific days of the week, etc.) and the exact times for each dose.
    - **Notes:** Add special instructions (e.g., "Take with food," "Avoid sunlight").
3.  Tap **"Save"** to activate reminders.

### Editing or Deleting a Medication

1.  Go to your **Medication List** (often a pill icon tab).
2.  Tap on the medication you wish to modify.
3.  Select **"Edit"** to change any details or **"Delete"** to remove it entirely.

## 4. Logging Doses & Reminders

### Responding to a Reminder

When a reminder alert appears on your phone:

1.  **Tap the Notification** to open the app.
2.  You will be presented with three options:
    - **TAKEN:** Confirms you've taken the dose. It will be logged in your history.
    - **SKIP:** Logs the dose as intentionally skipped.
    - **SNOOZE:** Delays the reminder.

### Snooze Feature

If you cannot take your medication immediately, tap **"Snooze."** You will be reminded again after a preset time (default is 10 minutes). The snooze duration can be changed in **Settings > Notification Settings**.

### Manual Logging

To log a dose outside of a reminder:
1.  On the Dashboard, find the medication.
2.  Tap the **"Log"** or **"+"** button next to it.
3.  Select the status (`Taken`, `Skipped`) and confirm.

## 5. History & Reports

View your adherence trends over time.

1.  Navigate to the **"History"** or **"Reports"** tab (often a calendar or chart icon).
2.  **View by Month/Week:** See a calendar view with color-coded days (Green for 100% adherence, etc.).
3.  **Adherence Rate:** Check your percentage of taken doses versus total expected doses.
4.  **Export:** You can generate a PDF report to share with your doctor during appointments.

---

## 6. App Settings

Access settings via the gear icon (⚙️), usually in the top corner or bottom tab bar.

### Notification Settings
- **Sound:** Choose your preferred alert tone.
- **Vibration:** Toggle vibration on/off.
- **Snooze Duration:** Set the default snooze time (5, 10, 15, 30 minutes).
- **Advanced Scheduling:** Enable/disable reminders for specific days.

### Data Management
- **Data Export:** Export all your data for your personal records.
- **App Reset:** Clear all your data and start fresh.

---

## 7. Tips for Success

- **Be Consistent:** Respond to reminders promptly to keep your history accurate.
- **Use Notes:** Note down side effects or questions for your doctor in the medication's "Notes" section.
- **Review Your Reports:** Before a doctor's appointment, check your history to discuss your adherence.
- **Regular Backups:** Since your data is stored locally, consider using the export feature periodically to save a copy of your medication history.

## 8. Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **No reminders?** | 1. Check phone "Do Not Disturb" mode.<br>2. Verify Dosezy has notification permissions in phone Settings.<br>3. Check Battery Saver settings, which may restrict background apps. |
| **App is slow/crashing** | 1. Close and restart the app.<br>2. Restart your phone.<br>3. Check for an app update in the store.<br>4. Reinstall the app (note: this will delete all your data as it's stored locally). |
| **Wrong medication in database** | You can always manually type the name and details. Our database is a guide, not a replacement for your prescription. |
| **Changing phones** | Your data is stored locally on your device. To transfer your data, use the **Export** feature before uninstalling the app on your old device, and then import the file on your new device. |
