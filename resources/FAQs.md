# Dosezy: Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

> **App Version:** v2.3.1

Welcome to the **Dosezy FAQ**! Here you will find clear, easy-to-understand answers to common questions about using Dosezy.

---

## 🌟 Getting Started & Privacy

### What is Dosezy?
Dosezy is a simple, friendly, and smart medication reminder app designed for everyday use. It helps you, your family members, and caregivers manage daily medicines, set alarms, track tablet stock, monitor adherence, and ensure no dose is ever forgotten.

### Do I need to create an account or connect to the internet?
No! Dosezy is **100% local-first and offline**. You do not need to register an account, log in, or have an internet connection. All your medicines and logs stay privately stored on your phone.

### Does Dosezy support my language?
Yes! Dosezy includes full native translations in **12 languages**:
- 🇬🇧 English, 🇪🇸 Spanish, 🇮🇳 Hindi, 🇨🇳 Chinese, 🇵🇹 Portuguese, 🇸🇦 Arabic, 🇫🇷 French, 🇩🇪 German, 🇯🇵 Japanese, 🇷🇺 Russian, 🇮🇹 Italian, and 🇧🇩 Bengali.

The app automatically opens in your phone's system language, and you can also switch languages anytime in **Menu > Preferences**.

## 💊 Adding & Managing Medications

### How do I add a new medicine?
1. Open the app and tap **Medicines** in the bottom bar, then tap **"+ Add Medicine"** (or the floating **"+"** button).
2. Enter your medicine name and select your dose unit (`tablet`, `capsule`, `mg`, `mcg`, `g`, `mL`, `drops`, `puffs`, `units`).
3. (Optional) Tap the camera icon to take a photo of your pill box or choose an image from your gallery.
4. Select a meal instruction (🍽️ *Before Food*, 🍲 *With Food*, 🥗 *After Food*, or ⏱️ *No Food Instructions*).
5. Choose your frequency: **Daily**, **Weekly** (select specific days of the week), or **Monthly** (select dates).
6. Tap on **1x, 2x, 3x, or 4x Daily** quick preset chips to automatically populate multiple dose times.
7. Tap any time chip to adjust it with the easy **12-First Grid Time Picker** (where 12 is placed at the top).
8. (Optional) Enter your current tablet count and low-stock refill threshold.
9. Tap **Save Medicine**.

## ⏰ Alarms, Grouped Doses & Status Colors

### What happens if I take multiple medicines at the same time?
Dosezy automatically groups medicines scheduled for the exact same minute (e.g., 8:00 AM) into a single alarm screen. You will see a checklist with pill names, dosages, and photos, along with a green **"Take All"** button and an orange **"Snooze (10 min)"** button, avoiding annoying overlapping alarms.

### How do dose status colors work on the Home screen?
Under each medication on the Home screen, status colors show your progress:
- **Green ("Taken")**: You confirmed taking your dose (shows exact time taken).
- **Standard Text (`To be taken in...` / `Xh Xm ago`)**: Dose is upcoming or due recently (within your "Consider Late After" window).
- **Orange (`Xh Xm ago`)**: Dose is **Late** (3 to 6 hours past due by default). An orange **"Mark Late"** button appears.
- **Red (`Xh Xm ago`)**: Dose is **Missed** (more than 6 hours past due by default).

### Can I customize when a dose is considered "Late" or "Missed"?
Yes! Go to **Menu > Preferences**:
- **Consider Late After**: Choose 1 hour, 2 hours, or 3 hours (default is 3 hours).
- **Consider Missed After**: Choose between 3 hours and 9 hours (default is 6 hours).
- **Default Snooze Duration**: Choose 5, 10, 15, 20, or 30 minutes.

### What if alarms or reminders are not sounding on my phone?
1. Open **Menu > Notification Status** (or tap the bell icon on the top bar) to see a live diagnostic checklist.
2. Ensure **Notifications** and **Exact Alarms** are enabled in Android settings.
3. Allow Dosezy to ignore battery optimization so Android does not delay alarms when your screen is locked.

## 📦 Pill Stock Inventory & Refill Warnings

### How does tablet stock tracking work?
When adding or editing a medicine:
1. Turn on **Track Stock Inventory**.
2. Enter your **Current Stock** (e.g., `60` pills) and **Refill Warning Threshold** (e.g., `10` pills).
3. Every time you mark a dose as **Taken**, Dosezy automatically deducts your dosage count from your stock.
4. When stock reaches or falls below your threshold, an orange **⚠️ Refill Warning: X left** badge appears on your Home screen and Medicines list.

## 📊 Calendar & Adherence Analytics

### How do I check past days or upcoming schedules?
Tap the **Schedule** tab at the bottom to view the interactive day and month calendar. Colored adherence dots on each calendar day indicate whether past doses were completed on time or missed.

### Where can I see my adherence percentage score?
Go to **Menu > Analytics**. You will see your overall adherence percentage ring (e.g. `92% Adherence`), totals of on-time vs late vs missed doses, and compliance breakdowns across **Week**, **Month**, **Year**, and **All Time**.

## 🚑 Emergency Calling & Personal Contacts

### How do I call emergency services or personal contacts?
1. Open **Menu > Emergency Contacts**.
2. **Official Emergency Services**: Dosezy automatically detects your country (India, USA/Canada, UK, EU, China, Japan, Russia, Brazil, Bangladesh, Australia) to dial ambulance, fire, or police in 1 tap.
3. **Personal Emergency Contacts**: Tap **"+ Add Emergency Contact"** to save your family doctor, daughter/son, or caregiver's phone number for instant 1-tap dialing.

## 👨‍👩‍👧 Family Profiles & Doctor Reports

### Can I manage medicines for multiple family members?
Yes! Tap **Menu > Switch Profile** to create separate profiles for parents, children, or partners. Each profile tracks its own medicines, dosages, stock counts, and compliance logs completely independently.

### How do I share my medicine logs with my doctor?
1. Go to **Menu > Export Data**.
2. Choose **PDF Report** to generate a clean, printable medical report with current prescriptions, dosages, food instructions, and adherence logs.
3. Tap **Export** and share it via WhatsApp, email, or save it to your phone.
4. You can also export a **JSON Backup** or **CSV** file for personal digital backups.

## ☁️ Cloud Sync & Caregiver Sharing (Roadmap)

### Can I sync my medicines to a web portal or share live with a caregiver?
In the current version, Dosezy is **100% offline and private** to guarantee zero data leaks and work without internet. 
An optional, self-hostable sync server and a dedicated **Caregiver Web Portal** are planned for the upcoming **v3.x** roadmap. Cloud sync will always remain strictly optional—you will always be able to use Dosezy completely offline.
