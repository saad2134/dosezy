# Dosezy: Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## Getting Started

### What is Dosezy?
Dosezy is a smart medication management app that helps you never miss a dose. It sends you timely reminders and tracks your adherence to help you and your healthcare provider manage your health effectively.

### How do I set up my first medication?
1.  Open the app and tap the "+" or "Add Medication" button.
2.  Enter the medication name. You can often search our database for auto-completion.
3.  Input the dosage (e.g., 500mg, 1 tablet).
4.  Set your schedule: choose the days and times you need to take it.
5.  Save, and your first reminder will be set!

### Is my health data secure and private?
Yes. We take your privacy and data security extremely seriously. All personal health information is stored securely on your device. We adhere to strict data protection regulations and do not sell your personal data. 

## Features & Usage

### How do medication reminders work?
You set the specific days and times for each medication. Dosezy will then send you a push notification at those scheduled times. 

### Can I track medications that I don't take daily (e.g., weekly or as needed)?
Absolutely. When setting up your schedule, you can select specific days of the week (e.g., every Monday and Thursday) or set a medication to be taken "As Needed" (PRN) without a fixed schedule.

### Can I share my medication history with my doctor?
Yes. Dosezy allows you to export your data. You can share these via email, text, or print them to bring to your medical appointments.

## Troubleshooting

### I'm not receiving my reminder notifications. What should I do?
1.  **Check App Permissions:** Ensure Dosezy has permission to send notifications in your phone's Settings.
2.  **Do Not Disturb:** Make sure your phone is not in "Do Not Disturb" or "Silent" mode.
3.  **Battery Optimization:** Some phones aggressively kill background apps. Check your battery settings and add Dosezy to the "unoptimized" or "allow background activity" list.
4.  **Restart the App:** Fully close and reopen the Dosezy app.
5.  **Restart Your Phone:** A simple reboot can often resolve notification issues.

### The app is crashing or running slowly. How can I fix this?
1.  **Update the App:** Download the latest .apk for any available updates.
2.  **Restart Your Phone:** This clears temporary memory and can resolve many performance issues.
3.  **Reinstall the App:** As a last resort, uninstall and then reinstall Dosezy. **Please ensure you have backed up your data first (the app offers this feature), to prevent data loss.**

## Account & Data

### Is Dosezy free to use?
Dosezy offers a robust free version. We may also offer a premium subscription with advanced features like detailed analytics, family sharing, and cloud backup. Check the "Subscription" section within the app for the latest plans.

### What happens to my data if I delete the app?
All your data is stored locally on your device and will be permanently lost if you delete the app. 

### How can I delete my account and all my data?
To permanently delete your account and all associated data, please go to the Settings menu within the app, find the "Manage Account" section, and select "Delete Account." This action is irreversible.
