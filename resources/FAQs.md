## 🌟 Getting Started

### What is Dosezy?
Dosezy is a simple, friendly, and smart medication reminder app. It helps you, your family members, and caregivers manage daily medicines, set reminders, and ensure no dose is ever missed.

### Does Dosezy support my language?
Yes! Dosezy v2.0.0 supports **12 major world languages**:

- 🇪🇸 Spanish (Español)
- 🇮🇳 Hindi (हिन्दी)
- 🇨🇳 Chinese (中文)
- 🇵🇹 Portuguese (Português)
- 🇸🇦 Arabic (العربية)
- 🇫🇷 French (Français)
- 🇩🇪 German (Deutsch)
- 🇯🇵 Japanese (日本語)
- 🇷🇺 Russian (Русский)
- 🇮🇹 Italian (Italiano)
- 🇧🇩 Bengali (বাংলা)

The app automatically opens in your phone's language, and you can also switch to any language at any time in **Preferences**.

### How do I add a new medicine?
1. Open the app and tap **Medicines** at the bottom, then tap the **"+"** button (or tap **Add Medicine**).
2. Type your medicine name and choose unit (e.g. `mg`, `mL`, `tablet`, `capsule`).
3. Set your schedule (Daily, Weekly on specific days, or Monthly).
4. Select your dose time using the easy **12-First Grid Time Picker**.
5. Tap **Save Medicine**.

## ⏰ Reminders, Color Badges & Late Tracking

### How do dose status colors work on the Home screen?
Dosezy uses clear colors under each medicine name so you know your exact status:
- **Normal Color (`Xh Xm ago`)**: Your medicine time passed recently (under your "Consider Late After" setting).
- **Orange (`Xh Xm ago`)**: Your medicine is **Late** (between 3 and 6 hours past due, or based on your preference setting). You will see an orange **"Mark Late"** button.
- **Red (`Xh Xm ago`)**: Your medicine is **Missed** (more than 6 hours past due, or based on your preference setting).
- **Green / Primary ("Taken")**: You have taken your dose!

### What does the text below the main time header (e.g. under 08:00 AM) mean?
Below each time slot header, Dosezy shows normal text showing how much time is remaining (`To be taken in 1h 30m`) or how long ago that time passed (`1h 15m ago`).

### Can I customize when a dose is considered "Late" or "Missed"?
Yes! Go to **Menu > Preferences**:
- **Consider Late After**: Choose 1 hour, 2 hours, or 3 hours (default is 3 hours).
- **Consider Missed After**: Choose between 3 hours and 9 hours (default is 6 hours).

## 🚑 International Emergency Calling

### How do I call emergency services in my country?
1. Open the **Menu** tab and tap **Emergency Contacts**.
2. Tap the **Region / Country** selector at the top.
3. Choose your country (India 🇮🇳, USA/Canada 🇺🇸🇨🇦, UK 🇬🇧, European Union 🇪🇺, China 🇨🇳, Japan 🇯🇵, Russia 🇷🇺, Brazil 🇧🇷, Bangladesh 🇧🇩, Australia 🇦🇺).
4. Tap on **Ambulance**, **Fire**, **Police**, or **Universal Emergency** to instantly dial the number on your phone!

## 👨‍👩‍👧 Family Profiles & Data Export

### Can I manage medicines for multiple family members?
Yes! Tap **Menu > Switch Profile** to create separate profiles for parents, children, or partners. Each profile tracks its own medicines and adherence logs independently.

### How do I share my medicine logs with my doctor?
1. Go to **Menu > Export Data**.
2. Choose **PDF Report** (for printing or emailing to your doctor) or **JSON Backup** (for saving a full backup file).
3. Tap **Export** and share it via email, WhatsApp, or drive.

## 🔒 Privacy & Safety

### Is my health information kept private?
Yes. 100% of your medicine data and personal health information stays stored safely on your own phone in an offline database. Dosezy does not sell or send your private medical data to external servers.
