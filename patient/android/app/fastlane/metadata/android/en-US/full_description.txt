A comprehensive, accessible medicine tracking and notifier app designed for stress-free medication management. Once you input the basics—name, dosage, timing, and frequency—Dosezy intelligently handles your medication schedule with zero privacy compromises.

✨ Key Features in Version 2.0.0:

• 🌐 12 Global Languages – Full native localization in English, Chinese (中文), Spanish (Español), Hindi (हिन्दी), Portuguese (Português), Arabic (العربية), French (Français), German (Deutsch), Japanese (日本語), Russian (Русский), Italian (Italiano), and Bengali (বাংলা).
• 🌍 International Emergency Contacts – Quick country & region selector supporting India, USA/Canada, UK, EU, China, Japan, Russia, Brazil, Bangladesh, and Australia with automatic country detection based on SIM/network country and system locale.
• ⏰ Customizable Late & Missed Thresholds – Configure "Consider Late After" (1–3 hours) and "Consider Missed After" (3–9 hours) preferences with high-visibility orange late indicators ("Xh Xm ago").
• 🕒 12-First Interactive Grid Time Picker – Convenient 12-hour (12 first layout) and 24-hour time selector with quick minute chips.
• ♿ Accessibility-First Design – High-contrast colors, clear typography, and simplified navigation for elderly users and multi-prescription management.
• 🔔 Reliable Notification System – Persistent alarm notifications with battery and sound state checks.
• 🎨 Dark & Light Theme System – Full adaptive theme support with zero white flash transitions.
• 📅 Flexible Scheduling – Daily, weekly, and monthly custom schedule patterns.
• 👤 Multi-Profile Support – Create, edit, and switch between family profiles easily.
• 🔒 Offline Data Security & Native Exports – Local Room database with printable PDF health reports, JSON backup archives, CSV data exports, organized Network/Data menu sections, and multi-store update checks.
• 👨‍👩‍👧 Caregiver Tools – Detailed adherence logs and missed dose history.
