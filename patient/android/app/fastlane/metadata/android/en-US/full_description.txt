Dosezy is an accessible, offline-first medication tracking and reminder app designed for stress-free prescription management. Input your medicines, dosages, and schedules, and Dosezy handles the rest—keeping your health on track with zero privacy compromises.

✨ KEY FEATURES:

• 🔔 Critical Full-Screen Alarms – High-visibility alarm screen rings reliably even in deep sleep or silent mode. Intercept with hardware volume keys to instantly silence audio, or use the back gesture to safely snooze.
• 🎵 Customizable Sound & Duration – Choose custom ringtones or built-in alert tones with in-app audio previews, dedicated alarm volume controls, and adjustable alarm durations (30 seconds to 5 minutes or continuous looping).
• ⏰ Dynamic Snooze & Time Windows – Flexible snooze presets (5, 10, 15, 20, or 30 minutes). Configure custom "Consider Late After" (1–3h) and "Consider Missed After" (3–9h) thresholds with live countdown badges.
• 📝 Clinical Dose Skipping – Opt-in dose skipping with mandatory clinical reasons (doctor advised, adverse reactions, fasting, illness). Skipped doses do not penalize your adherence streak and can be undone at any time.
• ↩️ Instant Intake Undo – Accidental taps can be reversed immediately with a 5-second undo snackbar or directly from the dose card, restoring deducted stock automatically.
• 📦 Pill Stock Inventory & Refill Warnings – Real-time stock tracking with automatic deduction on intake. Configurable low-stock alerts notify you when it is time to refill prescriptions.
• 🛒 Pharmacy Refill Order Generator – Instantly compile prescription refill orders from your active inventory and low-stock medicines. Calculate required supplies for 15, 30, 60, or 90 days, preview the formatted order, and share directly via chat or copy to clipboard.
• 📅 Interactive Schedule Calendar – Week and month calendar views with color-coded compliance dots and complete TalkBack screen reader accessibility announcing dose statuses.
• 📊 Adherence Analytics – Comprehensive compliance tracking across Today, 7 Days, 30 Days, 90 Days, and All-Time. Multi-ring breakdowns and individual medication adherence rates.
• 🔍 Real-Time Medicine Search – Quickly filter large medication rosters by name or instruction with instant empty-state feedback.
• 👥 Multi-Family Profiles – Manage medicines for multiple family members on one device. Each profile keeps separate medicines, schedules, inventory, and doctor contacts.
• 🔒 Full ZIP Backup & Selective Restore – Export complete encrypted backup archives. Restore individual profiles with granular conflict resolution: Create New, Merge Records, or Replace.
• 📄 Printable Doctor PDF Reports – Generate clean medical adherence reports with active prescriptions, dosages, food instructions, and compliance summaries for appointments.
• 🚨 International Emergency Dialing – 1-tap dialing for official emergency services across 10 regions with auto-detection, plus private personal emergency contacts per profile.
• 🌐 12 Native Languages – Full localization in English, Spanish, Hindi, Chinese, Portuguese, Arabic, French, German, Japanese, Russian, Italian, and Bengali.
• 🛡️ Notification Reliability Shield – Built-in diagnostic tool checks exact alarm permissions, battery optimization, and notification channels with manufacturer-specific setup guides.
• 🔒 100% Private & Offline – No accounts, no sign-ups, no tracking. All medical data stays encrypted locally on your phone.
