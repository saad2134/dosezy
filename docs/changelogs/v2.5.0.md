# `v2.5.0`

> **⚠️ Note for existing GitHub APK users**:
> Because Dosezy v2.4.2+ transitions to our official production release signing key (required for Google Play), updating directly over older debug-signed APKs requires a quick backup & reinstall:
> 1. In Dosezy, go to **Menu → Backup & Restore** and tap **Export/Create Backup**.
> 2. Uninstall the older version.
> 3. Install the new **v2.5.0** APK.
> 4. Go to **Menu → Backup & Restore** and tap **Restore Backup** to restore all profiles, medicines, and history in 1 tap.

## 💊 Patient App (Android)

* **🛒 Dosezy Pharmacy Refill Order Generator**:
  - **Instant Prescription Order Generator**: Integrated a dedicated refill order compiler under **Menu > Data Management > Pharmacy Refill Order** to quickly generate prescription reorder lists for pharmacies.
  - **Smart Duration & Inventory Math**: Select `15 Days`, `30 Days`, `60 Days`, or `90 Days` supply; Dosezy automatically computes required quantities based on daily dose frequencies and remaining stock.
  - **Filter Prescriptions**: Filter by **Low Stock Only** (showing only medicines at or under refill threshold, strictly excluding untracked items) or view **All Active** medications.
  - **Live Preview & 1-Tap Sharing**: Displays a formatted markdown preview with dosage instructions, ready to **Share via Chat** (WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS, etc.) or **Copy to Clipboard** with haptic confirmation.
  - **12-Language Support**: Fully localized across all 12 supported languages.

* **🔔 Alarm & Physical Control Enhancements**:
  - **Hardware Volume Rocker Silence**: Pressing either Volume Up or Volume Down while an alarm is ringing instantly silences audio and stops vibration without dismissing the alarm or requiring screen unlock.
  - **Back-Gesture Snooze**: Using the physical or gesture Back button on an active alarm automatically triggers a safe snooze for your configured duration.
  - **Dynamic Snooze Intervals**: Full-screen alarms, heads-up notifications (HUN), and background receivers now dynamically respect custom snooze preferences (5, 10, 15, 20, or 30 minutes).
  - **Customizable Alarm Duration & Volume**: Configure alarm ring lengths (30s, 1m, 2m, 3m, 5m, or continuous loop) and dedicated alarm volume.
  - **Notification Reliability Shield**: Streamlined system diagnostics with exact alarm guidance, battery optimization bypass checks, and direct OEM setup links (Samsung, Xiaomi, OnePlus, etc.).
  - **100% Alarm & Home Localization**: Replaced all hardcoded English strings on full-screen alarms, notification buttons, medication cards ("Take", "Taken", "Mark Late", "Missed"), and frequency dropdowns across all 12 supported languages.

* **📝 Intake Undo & Clinical Dose Skipping**:
  - **Instant Intake Undo**: Added a 5-second action Snackbar with "Undo" when marking doses taken, plus a persistent "Undo" button on medication cards to revert status back to Pending and safely restore deducted stock.
  - **Clinical Dose Skipping**: Opt-in ability under Preferences to pause doses with mandatory clinical reasons (*Doctor advised pause*, *Side effects / adverse reaction*, *Fasting*, *Illness*, *Ran out of medicine*). Skipped doses do not penalize adherence streaks.
  - **Daily Completion Banner & Schedule Review**: Replaced full-screen takeover with a celebratory `AllGoodBanner` pinned at the top when all doses are taken, keeping the entire day's schedule visible with interactive card buttons to review or undo taken doses.

* **💊 Medication & Schedule Management**:
  - **Real-Time Medicine Search**: Added an instant search and filter bar to `MedicinesScreen` allowing rapid searching across large rosters by name or instructions, plus an explicit "Add Your First Medication" primary CTA on empty lists.
  - **Safe Archived Medicine Editing**: Resolved a crash (`NullPointerException`) when opening or discontinuing archived medications in `EditMedScreen` by searching across active and archived rosters and safeguarding deletions.
  - **Form Validation Feedback**: Replaced silent button gray-outs in Add/Edit Medication with helpful validation guidance when required fields are incomplete.
  - **Strict Chronological Ordering**: Enforced chronological ordering (`ORDER BY scheduledDateTime ASC`) on daily and calendar schedule queries so doses appear in sequential morning-to-night sequence.

* **👥 Multi-Profile, Backup & Security Polish**:
  - **Namespaced Emergency Contacts**: Namespaced emergency contacts per user profile in private preferences (`contacts_json_<userId>`), preventing profile data leakage in multi-user households.
  - **Emergency Backup Integration**: Included emergency contacts directly in the backup ZIP archive for complete migration across devices.
  - **Profile Import Dialog Layout Polish**: Fixed text clipping on conflict resolution pills ("Merge Data" / "Overwrite") and action buttons ("Import Selected (1)" vs "Cancel") across all screen sizes.
  - **Android 13+ Language Switching**: Fixed activity recreation double-trigger on API 33+ devices that previously caused the app to exit to the home launcher upon language change.
  - **Preferences & Menu Reorganization**: Structured Preferences into General, Notifications & Alarms, and Dose Tracking sections with native subheadings across all 12 languages.

* **⚡ Lifecycle & Performance Fixes**:
  - **Schedule Calendar Date-Tap Optimization**: Deduplicated date change queries and cached monthly calendar indicator dots, eliminating rapid skeleton loader flashing when switching days.
  - **Cold-Start Home Screen Glitch**: Added a smooth loading gateway destination in `MainActivity.kt`, eliminating the brief flash of an empty home screen on fresh installs.
  - **F-Droid Build Compliance**: Refined release build signing configuration to cleanly default to unsigned APK when `keystore.properties` is absent.
