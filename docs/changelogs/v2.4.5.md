# `v2.4.5`

> **⚠️ Note for existing GitHub APK users**:
> Because Dosezy v2.4.2+ transitions to our official production release signing key (required for Google Play), updating directly over older debug-signed APKs requires a quick backup & reinstall:
> 1. In Dosezy, go to **Menu → Backup & Restore** and tap **Export/Create Backup**.
> 2. Uninstall the older version.
> 3. Install the new **v2.4.5** APK.
> 4. Go to **Menu → Backup & Restore** and tap **Restore Backup** to restore all profiles, medicines, and history in 1 tap.

## 💊 Patient App (Android)

* **Smart Default Medication Schedule Time**:
  - Initial scheduled time when creating a new medicine in **Add Medication** now defaults to **Current Time + 30 minutes** instead of a static morning hour.
  - Multi-dose daily presets (1x, 2x, 3x, 4x) now dynamically adapt starting from the +30 minute initial offset across the day.
* **Unified Alarm Engine & Active App (WhatsApp) Reliability**:
  - Centralized all playback into `AlarmAudioPlayer` singleton as the sole authority for alarm audio & vibration, completely eliminating duplicate/overlapping ringtones.
  - Silenced notification channel (`dosezy_medicine_reminders_v3`) and notification builder attachments so audio is exclusively managed by the alarm player.
  - Triggered audio and vibration immediately upon `MedicineAlarmReceiver` execution so custom audio plays even when another app (like WhatsApp) is active.
  - Added Android 14+ `ActivityOptions.MODE_BACKGROUND_ACTIVITY_START_ALLOWED` and an onboarding prompt for `SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW` ("Display over other apps") to ensure full-screen alarm popups show over active third-party apps.
  - Added direct alarm sound silencing and notification cancellation when tapping "Taken" or "Snooze" directly from Heads-Up Notification (HUN) banners.
