# `v2.4.3`

> **⚠️ Note for existing GitHub APK users**:
> Because Dosezy v2.4.2+ transitions to our official production release signing key (required for Google Play), updating directly over older debug-signed APKs requires a quick backup & reinstall:
> 1. In Dosezy, go to **Menu → Backup & Restore** and tap **Export/Create Backup**.
> 2. Uninstall the older version.
> 3. Install the new **v2.4.3** APK.
> 4. Go to **Menu → Backup & Restore** and tap **Restore Backup** to restore all profiles, medicines, and history in 1 tap.

---

## 💊 Patient App (Android)

* **Alarm Audio Stream Routing**:
  - Fixed custom alarm sound playback and preferences preview to route strictly through the system **Alarm volume stream** instead of the media volume stream before audio preparation.
  - Ensures alarms ring audibly at full alarm volume even when device media is muted or in silent media mode.
* **Full-Screen Popup & Screen Wakeup Reliability**:
  - Upgraded background alarm wake lock handling to standard `PARTIAL_WAKE_LOCK` with `ACQUIRE_CAUSES_WAKEUP` ensuring the full-screen reminder popup turns on the screen reliably when locked.
