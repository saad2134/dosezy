# v2.5.12: Retrospective Missed Dose Management and 48-Hour Clinical Integrity Guard

## Summary of Changes
Release v2.5.12 introduces retrospective dose management on the Schedule calendar screen, enabling patients to mark past missed doses as taken or skipped within a 48-hour clinical integrity window. This release maintains strict adherence and inventory synchronization by automatically decrementing medicine stock, tagging retrospective logs for doctor audit transparency, and locking older entries to preserve clinical history authenticity.

> **⚠️ Note for existing GitHub APK users**:
> Because Dosezy v2.4.2+ transitions to our official production release signing key (required for Google Play), updating directly over older debug-signed APKs requires a quick backup & reinstall:
> 1. In Dosezy, go to **Menu -> Backup & Restore** and tap **Export/Create Backup**.
> 2. Uninstall the older version.
> 3. Install the new **v2.5.12** APK.
> 4. Go to **Menu -> Backup & Restore** and tap **Restore Backup** to restore all profiles, medicines, and history in 1 tap.

## 📱 Patient App (Android)

* **Retrospective Missed Dose Management on Calendar (#109)**:
  - Enabled interactive dose actions on past calendar dates from the Schedule screen, resolving the issue where historical entries were entirely read-only.
  - Implemented an interactive action bottom sheet allowing patients to mark missed doses as taken, mark doses as skipped with a clinical reason, append or edit dose notes, or revert an accidental dose status.
  - Added smart time selector behavior in `RecordDoseTimeDialog` for past dates, defaulting intake time to the scheduled dose time and hiding "Just Now" to prevent recording yesterday's dose with today's timestamp.
* **48-Hour Clinical Integrity and Anti-Tampering Guard**:
  - Implemented a 48-hour clinical grace window that permits retrospective logging for yesterday and today while permanently locking entries older than 48 hours to prevent "white-coat compliance" record falsification before doctor consultations.
  - Displays a high-visibility locked indicator badge and explanatory message on calendar cards for doses outside the 48-hour window.
* **Automated Adherence Audit Tagging**:
  - Appends an automated `[Logged retrospectively]` audit tag to dose notes when a missed dose is marked as taken after the fact, ensuring full transparency in PDF export reports shared with physicians.
  - Automatically cleanses retrospective audit tags when an entry is reverted back to pending.
* **Inventory Stock Synchronization**:
  - Transitioning a historical missed dose to taken automatically decrements current medicine inventory stock, ensuring the physical pill bottle count matches the app.
  - Undoing a dose action restores the deducted stock back to inventory in an atomic transaction.
* **100% Translations Parity Across 12 Locales**:
  - Added complete, localized strings for retrospective management, dialog actions, locking notices, and snackbar confirmations across Arabic, Bengali, German, Spanish, French, Hindi, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese, Russian, and Chinese.
* **Slide to Snooze Duration Preference Fix (#113)**:
  - Fixed a defect where the "Slide to Snooze" confirmation button defaulted to 10 minutes and ignored user snooze duration settings due to stale pointer gesture callback capture.
  - Implemented `rememberUpdatedState` in `SlideToConfirmButton` to ensure pointer drag completions always invoke the latest selected duration.
  - Forwarded `EXTRA_SNOOZE_MINUTES` through `alarmIntent` to initialize the alarm activity UI with the user's configured duration immediately on frame 1 before database loading finishes.
