## 1. App Overview, 12 Languages & Dark/Light Themes

Dosezy is designed with accessibility and clarity as top priorities: large text, high-contrast buttons, streamlined navigation, and zero confusing menus.

### 12 Supported World Languages:
Dosezy is fully localized across all screens, notification actions, alarms, and settings in **12 languages**:
- 🇬🇧 English, 🇪🇸 Spanish (Español), 🇮🇳 Hindi (हिन्दी), 🇨🇳 Chinese (中文), 🇵🇹 Portuguese (Português), 🇸🇦 Arabic (العربية), 🇫🇷 French (Français), 🇩🇪 German (Deutsch), 🇯🇵 Japanese (日本語), 🇷🇺 Russian (Русский), 🇮🇹 Italian (Italiano), and 🇧🇩 Bengali (বাংলা).

### Changing Language, Theme, or Time Format:
1. Tap the **Menu** tab at the bottom right.
2. Tap **Preferences**.
3. Under the **General** section:
   - **Language**: Choose your preferred language.
   - **Theme**: Switch between **System Default**, **Light**, or **Dark** mode.
   - **Time Format**: Toggle between **12-Hour (AM/PM)** and **24-Hour** display.

## 2. Home Dashboard, Live Status Badges & Intake Undo

The **Home** screen displays all medications scheduled for today, grouped into clean chronological time sections (e.g., `08:00 AM`, `01:00 PM`, `08:00 PM`).

### Time Section Headers:
Below each time header, Dosezy displays real-time countdowns and time differences:
- `To be taken in 45m`: An upcoming dose.
- `1h 15m ago`: A dose whose time has passed.
- `All medications taken`: All pills in this time slot are completed.

### Medication Card Badges & Actions:
Each medicine card displays its name, dosage, meal instructions, stock status, and an interactive action button:
- 🟢 **Taken**: Green button with the recorded completion timestamp (e.g. `Taken at 08:05 AM`).
- ⚪ **Take**: Active dose button for upcoming or due medications.
- 🟧 **Mark Late**: Appears when a dose is past due but still within your late window.
- 🔴 **Missed**: Indicates a dose that passed your missed threshold window.

### Instant Intake Undo:
If you accidentally tap "Take" on a pill by mistake:
1. A 5-second snackbar immediately appears with an **"Undo"** action.
2. The card button also remains interactive with an enabled **"Undo"** action.
3. Tapping Undo immediately rolls back the dose status to **Pending** and automatically returns the deducted tablet count back to your stock inventory.

## 3. Daily Completion Banner & Full Schedule Review

When all medications scheduled for the day have been marked as taken:
- A cheerful **All Good** completion banner is displayed at the top of the screen.
- Crucially, **today's full medication list remains visible underneath** in a clean checked state.
- Patients and caregivers can review exact dosages, notes, and taken timestamps at any point during the day, or tap Undo if any dose was logged in error.

## 4. Adding, Editing, Searching & Archiving Medications

### Adding a Medication:
1. Tap **Medicines** in the bottom navigation bar.
2. Tap **"+ Add Medicine"** (or the **"Add Your First Medication"** button on an empty list).
3. Enter the **Medicine Name** (e.g., *Metformin*, *Aspirin*, *Lisinopril*).

### Live Medication Search & Filtering:
At the top of the **Medicines** screen, an instant search bar allows you to quickly filter large medication rosters in real time. Simply type any part of the medicine name or special instructions.

### Medicine Photos & Food Instructions:
- **Medicine Photo**: Tap the camera icon to photograph your pill box or choose an image from your gallery with built-in cropping.
- **Meal Instructions**: Choose between:
  - 🍽️ *Before Food*
  - 🍲 *With Food*
  - 🥗 *After Food*
  - ⏱️ *No Food Instructions*

### Dosage Units & Frequency Patterns:
- **Dosage**: Enter the quantity (e.g. `1`, `500`) and select the unit:
  - `tablet`, `capsule`, `mg`, `mcg`, `g`, `mL`, `drops`, `puffs`, or `units`.
- **Frequency Pattern**:
  - **Daily**: Every day.
  - **Weekly**: Choose specific days of the week (e.g., Mon, Wed, Fri).
  - **Monthly**: Choose specific dates of the month (e.g., 1st, 15th).
  - **As Needed (PRN)**: For pain relievers or temporary medications taken only when required.

### Multi-Dose Quick Presets (1x, 2x, 3x, 4x):
For medications taken multiple times per day, tap a quick preset chip to populate standard times automatically:
- **1x Daily**: `08:00 AM`
- **2x Daily**: `08:00 AM`, `08:00 PM`
- **3x Daily**: `08:00 AM`, `02:00 PM`, `08:00 PM`
- **4x Daily**: `08:00 AM`, `12:00 PM`, `04:00 PM`, `08:00 PM`

### 12-First Grid Time Picker:
Tap any dose time chip to open the intuitive time selector:
- **12 is at the top**: Hour numbers start with 12 followed by 1 to 11 for natural morning/night selection.
- **AM / PM Toggle**: 1-tap switching.
- **Quick Minute Chips**: Tap `:00`, `:15`, `:30`, or `:45`, or slide the minute bar for exact minute precision.

### Discontinuing & Archiving Medications:
When a prescription is completed or paused by your doctor:
1. Open the medication from the Medicines screen and tap **Edit**.
2. Tap **Discontinue / Archive**.
3. The medicine is moved to your **Archived Medications** roster. Its past calendar logs, historical compliance rates, and doctor reports are preserved permanently. You can reactivate the medication at any time.

### Form Validation Feedback:
When adding or editing a medication, Dosezy provides clear feedback if any required information is missing (such as selecting at least one weekday for weekly frequency, or entering a valid dosage number).

## 5. Grouped Alarms, "Take All" & Dynamic Snooze

When multiple medications are scheduled for the exact same minute:
- Dosezy rings with a **single grouped full-screen alarm** instead of multiple overlapping alarms.
- The screen displays an interactive checklist with pill names, dosages, and photos.
- **TAKE ALL (Green Button)**: Confirms all scheduled medicines in that slot with 1 tap and deducts stock automatically.
- **SNOOZE (Orange Button)**: Pauses the alarm for your chosen snooze duration.
- Individual pills can also be checked off one by one if taking only a subset.

### Dynamic Snooze Duration:
Dosezy supports flexible snooze durations. Go to **Menu > Preferences > Notifications & Alarms > Default Snooze Duration** and choose between:
- **5 minutes**, **10 minutes**, **15 minutes**, **20 minutes**, or **30 minutes**.
Your preferred duration is dynamically displayed on alarm popup buttons and notification actions.

## 6. Notification Reliability Shield & Diagnostics

To guarantee alarms ring reliably even in Android deep sleep:
- A persistent **Notification Reliability Shield** button is visible on the top bar across all main screens and subpages.
- When green, all system permissions are verified.
- Tapping the shield opens a live diagnostic checklist:
  - **Exact Alarms**: Verifies precision alarm dispatch.
  - **Notifications**: Verifies notification channel status.
  - **Battery Optimization**: Checks if background power restrictions are disabled.
- Direct links provide 1-tap access to your device's settings, including manufacturer-specific guides for Samsung, Xiaomi, Huawei, OnePlus, and Oppo devices.

## 7. Pill Inventory & Automatic Refill Warnings

Dosezy includes automatic inventory management so you never run out of critical medications:

1. In the Add/Edit Medicine screen, turn on **Track Stock Inventory**.
2. Enter your **Current Stock** count (e.g. `60` tablets).
3. Enter your **Refill Warning Threshold** (e.g. `10` tablets).
4. **Auto-Deduction**: Whenever you tap **Taken** (from alarms or the Home screen), Dosezy automatically subtracts your dosage amount from your stock.
5. **Low Stock Alerts**: When remaining stock reaches or drops below your threshold, an orange **`⚠️ Refill Warning: X left`** badge is displayed on your Home screen and Medicines list.

## 8. Interactive Schedule Calendar

Tap **Schedule** in the bottom navigation bar to view your full medication calendar:
- **Week & Month Views**: Tap any day (Mon–Sun) or date on the calendar to view past or future schedules.
- **Adherence Markers**: Colored dots on each calendar day show your adherence for that date (green for all taken, orange/red for missed doses).
- **Fast Date Switching**: Tapping any day instantly loads that date's chronological schedule.

## 9. Adherence Analytics & Progress Scores

Tap **Analytics** from the Menu to view detailed compliance charts:
- **Time Range Selector**: View stats for **WEEK**, **MONTH**, **YEAR**, or **ALL** time.
- **Overall Score Ring**: Displays your total adherence percentage (e.g. `92% Adherence`).
- **Dose Breakdown**: Counts of **On-Time**, **Late**, **Missed**, and **Total** doses.
- **Medicine Breakdown**: See compliance rates for each specific medicine to identify patterns.
- New profiles are greeted with an informative guide explaining how adherence streaks grow as doses are recorded.

## 10. Structured Preferences & Dose Tracking Settings

The **Preferences** screen is organized into dedicated categories:
- **General**: Language selection (12 languages), Theme (System/Light/Dark), Time Format (12h/24h).
- **Notifications & Alarms**: Default Snooze Duration (5–30 min), Custom Alarm Ringtone, Sound Volume.
- **Dose Tracking**:

2. **Official Emergency Services**:

     - 🇯🇵 **Japan** (119 / 110)

   - Tap **"+ Add Emergency Contact"** to save personal numbers (family doctor, primary caregiver, son, daughter, neighbor).
   - Emergency contacts are scoped per user profile, ensuring personal doctor numbers remain isolated between household members.

## 12. Managing Multiple Family Profiles

Manage medications for your whole household on a single phone:

1. Tap **Menu > Switch Profile**.
2. Tap **Add Profile** to create a profile for an elderly parent, child, or partner.
3. Enter their Name, Age, Gender, and optional Profile Picture.
4. Tap any profile card to switch active users. Tapping the currently selected profile returns smoothly to your dashboard. Each profile maintains its own separate medicine list, schedules, stock counts, personal emergency contacts, and adherence history.

---

## 13. Exporting Doctor PDF Reports & Encrypted Backups

Export health records for doctor visits or keep complete personal backups:

1. **Doctor PDF Report**: Go to **Menu > Export Data** and select **PDF Report** to generate a clean, printable medical report with current prescriptions, dosages, food instructions, and adherence logs.
2. **Encrypted Backups**:
   - Go to **Menu > Backup & Restore** and tap **Export Backup** to generate an encrypted ZIP archive containing all profiles, active & archived medicines, compliance logs, and emergency contacts.
   - When moving to a new phone, install Dosezy, tap **Restore Backup**, and choose whether to create new profiles, merge data, or overwrite existing profiles.
